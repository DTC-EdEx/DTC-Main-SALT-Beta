▶Leave of Absence

Requesting a Leave of Absence: Life happens. If an unforeseen event affects your ability to finish a course, a Leave of Absence (LOA) can pause your coursework and safely extend your end date.

QUICK CHECKLIST
  • Applies to: absences of 4 or more days in a row.
  • Timeframe: must be requested within 30 days of the conflict.
  • Documentation: fill out the LOA Request Form (linked below) with supporting documentation.
  • Work conflicts: skip the form, but email documentation to Student Services within 10 days.

Questions? Connect with Student Services. Your advisor can help you through the process.
