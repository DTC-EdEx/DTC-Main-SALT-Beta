Course Completion Instructions

Congratulations on completing your course! Here’s how to schedule your next one.

NEXT STEPS • SCHEDULE YOUR NEXT COURSE

Schedule your next course within 24 hours

To keep your momentum going, and to meet federal enrollment guidelines, please schedule your next course within 24 hours of finishing your current one.

GOOD TO KNOW

You don’t have to start class the next day! You just need an upcoming start date, within the next 5 business days, locked in on your schedule.

Follow these 3 steps

  1. Complete your course survey Share your feedback on the course you just finished. Fill out the course survey.
  2. Confirm your progress Check in with your instructor to make sure your official course completion is recorded.
  3. Submit your next schedule request Follow the step-by-step guide below to submit your Schedule Change Approval Form.

Questions about your schedule or requirements? Reach out to your Academic Advisor. We’re happy to help!
